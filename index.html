Free AI art (Pollinations)

Describe one cute illustration. Free AI art is hit-or-miss, so hit Regenerate until you like one. Family-friendly prompts only.

How to use AI art: the free service only lets your browser show the picture, not edit it. When you like one, save the image (right-click → Save image, or long-press on a phone), then upload it with ＋ Upload on the left; the white background is removed for you.
Heads up: free Pollinations images carry a small “pollinations.ai” logo in the corner, and their terms say to check each AI model's license before commercial use. For things you sell, the built-in art or your own licensed clipart is the safest choice.
